A property draws at least 200 inputs from a pseudo-random generator seeded with a fixed constant, at the full resolution of each type (to the microsecond for a time, below the smallest unit the specification names for an amount), and asserts what the requirement fixes for every drawn input.
Check only what the requirement's text, read under the specification's precedence, fixes. Never assert an output the specification leaves open or that another requirement decides.
Use the standard library, the public interface and the test helpers only.
